## 떡볶이 레시피 · English

검색 제목: Tteokbokki Recipe – Sweet and Spicy Korean Rice Cakes

검색 설명: Make sweet and spicy tteokbokki with chewy Korean rice cakes. Choose the right rice cakes, adjust the heat and simmer a glossy gochujang sauce.

Tteokbokki (떡볶이) is a Korean street-food dish of chewy rice cakes in a sweet and spicy sauce. This simple everyday version uses water, gochujang, gochugaru and soy sauce, with green onion added at the end. The recipe makes 2 servings and has a listed total time of 30 minutes.

### Which rice cakes should I buy?

Choose 300 g of plain, cylinder-shaped [tteokbokki rice cakes](https://www.hansikyoung.com/ingredients/tteokbokki-tteok). Look for tteokbokki-tteok or topokki rice cakes at a Korean or other Asian grocery store, or an online retailer delivering to your country. A ready-made kit may include its own sauce; this recipe uses plain rice cakes with the sauce ingredients listed below. Crispy puffed rice snacks and sweet filled rice cakes are different products.

### Preparing frozen or firm rice cakes

Follow the preparation instructions on your rice-cake packet. Frozen rice cakes may need thawing and a short soak before cooking; separate pieces that are stuck together. Fresh, refrigerated and frozen products do not all soften at the same speed. The rice cakes should become tender and chewy all the way through, rather than remaining hard in the center.

### Gochujang and gochugaru have different jobs

The sauce uses [gochujang paste](https://www.hansikyoung.com/ingredients/gochujang) for its sweet, spicy and savory flavor. Choose the paste rather than a bottle of ready-made dipping sauce, which may already contain other seasonings.

It also uses [gochugaru](https://www.hansikyoung.com/ingredients/gochugaru), Korean chili flakes or powder. These add chili flavor and heat but do not replace the paste spoon for spoon. Read the quantities in the ingredient checklist before adding either one.

### How can I make it less spicy?

Choose a milder gochujang and use less gochugaru if you prefer gentler heat. The result will differ from the listed recipe. Taste the sauce and adjust gradually: extra sugar makes it sweeter, but does not remove the chili already in the pan. Reducing gochujang also changes the sauce seasoning and body.

### What should the finished sauce look like?

The recipe starts with 360 ml water. Stir regularly while the rice cakes cook so they do not stick. If the sauce is watery, let it simmer and reduce until it lightly coats the rice cakes and looks glossy. If it becomes too thick while the centers are still hard, add a small splash of water and keep cooking gently. Judge both the sauce and the rice cakes before serving.

### Optional additions and serving

Serve straight away for the best soft, chewy texture. Eomuk, cabbage or boiled eggs are optional additions from the existing recipe; the basic ingredient list does not include them. More additions can change how much sauce you need, so keep the pan from drying out.

For other recipes using the same staples, see the [Korean pantry starter guide](https://www.hansikyoung.com/korean-pantry) and the ingredient pages linked above.

## 떡 재료 · English

검색 제목: Tteokbokki Rice Cakes – Buying and Preparation Guide

검색 설명: Choose plain tteokbokki rice cake sticks, learn how to prepare frozen or firm tteok and find the matching Korean rice cake recipe.

Tteokbokki-tteok (떡볶이 떡) are the plain, chewy Korean rice-cake sticks used in tteokbokki. They soften during cooking and hold the sweet, spicy sauce. They are different from crispy puffed rice snacks or sweet filled rice cakes.

### Choosing the right packet

Look for plain cylinder-shaped rice cakes labeled tteokbokki-tteok, topokki rice cakes or rice cake sticks. Compare the ingredient list, shape and package weight. Rice cakes sold with sauce in a ready-made meal kit are a different purchase from the plain ingredient used in this recipe.

### Where to find them

Try a Korean or other Asian grocery store, including its chilled or frozen sections. Some products are vacuum-packed. For online shopping, choose a food retailer that delivers to your country and check whether the product needs chilled or frozen delivery. Availability and storage instructions depend on the product.

### Preparing them for tteokbokki

Follow the instructions on your packet for thawing, rinsing or soaking. Separate pieces that are stuck together before cooking. Frozen or firm rice cakes can need more preparation than fresh ones, so a single soaking time will not suit every product.

The [Hansik Young tteokbokki recipe](https://www.hansikyoung.com/recipes/tteokbokki) uses 300 g for 2 servings. Cook until the centers are soft and chewy, stirring so the rice cakes do not stick to the pan. If the sauce reduces before they soften, add a small splash of water and continue cooking gently.

### Can I use another shape?

Thin rice-cake slices used in soup have a different shape and can cook differently. Plain tteokbokki sticks are the closest choice for the linked recipe. Noodles or crispy rice snacks do not give the same texture.

### Keeping the remaining rice cakes

Follow the storage instructions and dates on the package, including the instructions after opening. A sealed packet is not necessarily suitable for room-temperature storage. Keep chilled or frozen products under the conditions specified by the manufacturer.

## 떡 재료 · Deutsch

검색 제목: Tteokbokki-Reiskuchen: Kaufen und richtig vorbereiten

검색 설명: Tteokbokki-Reiskuchen auswählen, in Deutschland finden und vorbereiten: So erkennst du die passende Packung für das verlinkte Rezept.

Tteokbokki-Tteok (떡볶이 떡) sind die puren, bissfesten koreanischen Reiskuchen-Stäbchen für Tteokbokki. Beim Garen werden sie weich und elastisch und nehmen die süß-scharfe Sauce auf. Sie sind weder knusprige Reiswaffeln noch süß gefüllte Reiskuchen.

### Die passende Packung auswählen

Suche nach puren, zylinderförmigen Reiskuchen mit Bezeichnungen wie Tteokbokki-Tteok, Topokki oder Rice Cake Sticks. Vergleiche Zutatenliste, Form und Packungsgewicht. Ein Fertigset mit Sauce ist ein anderes Produkt als die puren Reiskuchen im verlinkten Rezept.

### In Deutschland finden

Schau in koreanischen oder anderen Asia-Lebensmittelgeschäften auch im Kühl- und Tiefkühlbereich nach. Manche Produkte sind vakuumverpackt. Bei Onlineshops mit Lieferung nach Deutschland solltest du prüfen, ob Kühl- oder Tiefkühlversand nötig ist. Verfügbarkeit und Lagerung hängen vom jeweiligen Produkt ab.

### Für Tteokbokki vorbereiten

Beachte die Packungshinweise zum Auftauen, Abspülen oder Einweichen. Trenne aneinanderhaftende Stücke vor dem Garen. Gefrorene oder feste Reiskuchen können mehr Vorbereitung brauchen als frische; eine einzige Einweichzeit passt deshalb nicht zu jeder Packung.

Das [Tteokbokki-Rezept auf Hansik Young](https://www.leckere-koreanische-rezepte.de/recipes/tteokbokki) verwendet 300 g für 2 Portionen. Gare die Reiskuchen, bis sie auch innen weich und elastisch sind, und rühre regelmäßig. Wenn die Sauce zu früh eindickt, gib einen kleinen Schuss Wasser dazu und gare vorsichtig weiter.

### Kann ich eine andere Form verwenden?

Dünne Reiskuchen-Scheiben für Suppen haben eine andere Form und können anders garen. Pure Tteokbokki-Stäbchen passen am besten zum verlinkten Rezept. Nudeln oder knusprige Reiswaffeln ergeben nicht dieselbe Konsistenz.

### Den Rest aufbewahren

Beachte die Lagerungshinweise und Datumsangaben auf der Packung, auch nach dem Öffnen. Eine versiegelte Packung darf nicht automatisch bei Raumtemperatur gelagert werden. Kühl- und Tiefkühlprodukte brauchen die vom Hersteller angegebenen Bedingungen.
